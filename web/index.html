FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything teams usually ask before moving their first shilling.

How fast do payments actually settle? ⌄

STK push acknowledgements return in under a second. The money settles the moment your customer enters their PIN — usually in 90 seconds end to end, confirmed by the webhook. Cross-border corridors settle within minutes.

What does the sandbox include? ⌄

Free sandbox keys with your own Daraja app, the shared test shortcode (174379), and the standard test passkey. You can run the whole STK push flow against the mock server in CI without touching the network.

Do I need a bank account or Paybill to start? ⌄

No. In sandbox you use Safaricom's shared shortcode. For production you link your own Paybill or Till — the Daraja team connects it to your app as part of go-live (1–3 business days).

What happens when a webhook callback is dropped? ⌄

Daraja retries failed callbacks, and our SDK also exposes an STK query fallback so you can poll for the settled state. Every event is idempotent, so retries never double-book your ledger.
